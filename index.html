Measured, not guessed

Most clinics judge progress by asking how it feels. We ask that too, then we measure your strength, balance and movement, using the same kind of force plates professional sports teams use where they’ll help. So you can see your recovery, not just hope it’s happening.

  1. Measure

    A full assessment plus starting measures of your strength, balance and movement.

  2. Treat and train

    Hands-on treatment and exercises aimed at exactly what we find.

  3. Re-test

    The same measures at your review, so you can see what’s changed.

Your questions answered

Do I need a GP referral?

No. You can book directly online or by phone. Many health insurers cover osteopathy, so it’s worth checking your policy.

What happens at my first appointment?

A detailed conversation about your symptoms, health and lifestyle, then an examination. We’ll explain what we find, agree a plan with you, and start treatment where it’s appropriate.

What should I wear?

We may need to see how you move, so wear or bring something comfortable such as shorts and a vest top. You’re welcome to bring someone with you.

What happens in a longevity assessment?

We measure your body composition, metabolic ageing, heart, lungs, strength and power. You receive a clear report explaining each result, and you can choose to have a personal plan written for you.
