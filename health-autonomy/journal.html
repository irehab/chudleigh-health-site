October 2026

The starting line

I’ve been an osteopath for 20 years, and I’ve spent a lot of that time telling people that stiffness, weakness and poor balance aren’t just “getting older”. Most of it can be improved. I created Health Autonomy to prove it, and this journal is me putting myself through exactly what I ask of patients.

I know recovery first-hand. After acute brachial neuritis and spondylolisthesis, I rebuilt my fitness and went on to complete the Roc Triathlon adventure race in Wales. Now I want to see what the numbers say.

This month I’m taking my baseline assessment: body composition, AGE Reader, ECG, heart rate variability, spirometry and force plates. My results will go up here as soon as I have them, good or bad, followed by my plan for the next 8 weeks.
