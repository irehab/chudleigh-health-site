What we measure

Six tests. One clear picture.

One 45-minute appointment. No needles.

Body composition

Tanita Segmental

Painless scan to review biometric data including muscle and fat in each arm, leg and your trunk, plus visceral fat, body water and phase angle.

Metabolic health

Autofluorescence Scan

A painless light scan measuring tissue advanced glycation end-products (AGEs), reflecting dietary and metabolic impact.

Heart

12-lead ECG

A resting baseline of your heart rate and rhythm before you start training, so you know where you stand.

Recovery & stress

HRV · Frontier X2

A light chest strap worn during your session shows how well you balance stress and recovery, measuring real-time heart rate variability (HRV).

Lungs

Spirometry & SpO2

Airflow capacity, lung age and blood oxygen saturation (SpO2). A snapshot of how well your lungs and circulation deliver oxygen to your body.

Power & balance

Force Plates

Muscular power and left/right balance. Reveals hidden compensations and asymmetries; paired with Tanita data to show if a weaker side lacks muscle, power or both.

VO2max
Included in every package

Plus your calculated VO2 max

One of the strongest predictors of long-term health, and the number longevity experts and athletes care about most. Calculated from your assessment data, so no exhausting treadmill test.

Good to know

Questions people ask

How should I prepare for the assessment?
  • No large meal, alcohol or caffeine for 2–3 hours.
  • No smoking for 1 hour, and no hard exercise that day.
  • No creams, sunscreen or fake tan on your forearms.
  • Wear clothes you can squat and move in.
Is the assessment suitable for everyone?

Most adults can take part. Tell us when booking if you have a pacemaker or implanted device, are pregnant, or have had recent surgery or a heart problem, and we'll adapt the session.

Is this a medical diagnosis?

No. This is a health and fitness baseline, not a medical diagnosis. If any of the reports need additional checking, we'll advise you of the next step.

I'm already fit. Is this for me?

Yes. The tests are the same for everyone, but how we interpret them and what we recommend depends on your goals. For athletes, force plate power and asymmetry, segmental body composition and heart data help pinpoint where the extra gains are.

I'm not very active. Is it too much for me?

Not at all. The movements are simple and adapted to you, and your plan starts from where you are today.

Does it hurt? Are there needles?

No needles and no blood tests. You'll stand on the Tanita, rest your arm on a scanner, lie down for a heart trace, wear a light chest strap, breathe into a spirometer and do a few simple movements on the force plates.
