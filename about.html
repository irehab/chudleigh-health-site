About Chudleigh Health Hub

At Chudleigh Health Hub, we’re committed to providing outstanding osteopathy and physiotherapy in Chudleigh. Our team puts your health first, with a whole-person approach to treatment and care.

We believe in empowering our patients through personalised care and therapies, giving you the knowledge to look after your own health rather than only treating you when something goes wrong.

Our new name, Health Autonomy

Health Autonomy is the next chapter for Chudleigh Health Hub. The name reflects what we believe: you should understand your body and feel in control of your health. You’ll still find us in the same place, with the same team.

Training the next generation

John leads the Osteopathic Medicine master’s programme at Plymouth Marjon University, and Steph and Will lecture there too. It keeps our own practice up to date and held to a high standard.

Meet the team

Our osteopaths and physiotherapist work together, so you get the right care for your problem.

John Evans

Registered Osteopath, GOsC 6171

John graduated in 2006 and has dedicated his career to longevity and health, exploring how to improve wellbeing and extend healthy life. He is Programme Leader for the Osteopathic Medicine master’s programme at Plymouth Marjon University, where he teaches neuromusculoskeletal conditions and new approaches to treatment. He’s passionate about sharing knowledge so people can live healthy, active lives.

Steph Evans

Registered Osteopath, GOsC 6208

Steph graduated in 2006 and specialises in musculoskeletal conditions. With a deep knowledge of the body, she builds personalised treatment plans to help each patient reach their best health. She is committed to compassionate care and is an experienced lecturer at Plymouth Marjon University, where she works with John to educate future osteopaths.
